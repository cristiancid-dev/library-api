# Business Rules

## BR-01
A book cannot be loaned more than once at the same time.

## BR-02
A loan maximum duration it's 30 days.

## BR-03
If a loan exceeds the maximum duration, the member will be banned.

## BR-04
A banned member cannot borrow any book until the exceeded loan book is returned

## BR-05
A member cannot borrow more than 5 books at the same time.
